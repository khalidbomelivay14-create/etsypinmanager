Privacy Policy for EtsyPinManager

Last updated: October 20, 2026

EtsyPinManager helps its operator prepare Pinterest Pin drafts from publicly available Etsy product information and images. This policy explains what information the app may process and how it is handled.

Information processed

  • Product information: Etsy listing URLs and publicly accessible listing details, such as titles, descriptions, and image links, when available.
  • Uploaded files: Product images that the user chooses to upload.
  • Draft content: Pin images, titles, descriptions, destination links, and export files created by the user.
  • Pinterest account data: The current version does not sign in to Pinterest or access Pinterest account data. If Pinterest API integration is added in a future version, the app will request only the permissions needed for the features the user chooses and will update this policy as necessary.

How information is used

Information is used to import product details, create Pin drafts, and let the user download generated files. The current version does not publish Pins to Pinterest and does not sell personal information.

Storage and sharing

Information may be processed on the device or computer running the app and may be included in files the user exports. The app operator does not intentionally sell or rent personal information. Users should avoid uploading confidential information or images they do not have permission to use.

Third-party services

The app may access public Etsy listing pages to retrieve available listing information. Etsy and Pinterest are separate services and their own privacy policies and terms apply when you use their websites or services.

Data retention and deletion

Users can delete locally saved app files and exported files from their own device. Any future hosted service or Pinterest integration will describe applicable retention and deletion practices before launch.

Children's privacy

This app is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
